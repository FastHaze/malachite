# Design Brief

Part of [[Website Redesign]].

## Goals

1. Make the pricing page ==understandable in ten seconds==
2. Cut the number of pages by half
3. Load in under a second on a phone

## Audience

- Small teams choosing a tool for the first time
- People comparing us with a spreadsheet

## Constraints

- No CMS: content lives in Markdown ^no-cms
- One typeface, two weights
- Works without JavaScript

## Tone

Plain. Short sentences. <mark style="background: #E9314766;">No superlatives.</mark>
