# Kitchen Plan

Part of [[Home Renovation]].

## Layout

The sink stays under the window. The hob moves to the long wall, which needs a new circuit.

## Order of work

1. Strip out the old units
2. Electrician: new circuit, sockets above the worktop
3. Plumber: move the dishwasher feed
4. Plaster and paint
5. Units, then worktop, then tiles
