# Lentil Soup

Serves four. Forty minutes.

## Ingredients

- 250 g red lentils
- 1 onion, 2 carrots, 2 cloves of garlic
- 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 litre stock
- Juice of half a lemon

## Method

1. Soften the onion and carrot in oil, ten minutes
2. Add garlic and spices, one minute
3. Add lentils and stock, simmer twenty-five minutes
4. Blend half of it, stir back in, add the lemon

Good with [[Sourdough Bread]].
