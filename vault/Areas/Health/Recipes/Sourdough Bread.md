# Sourdough Bread

One loaf. A day, mostly waiting.

## Timetable

- [ ] 08:00 feed the starter
- [ ] 13:00 mix flour and water, rest an hour
- [ ] 14:00 add starter and salt
- [ ] 14:30 to 18:30 fold every hour
- [ ] 19:00 shape, into the fridge overnight
- [ ] next morning: bake 45 minutes at 240 °C

## Quantities

| Flour | Water | Starter | Salt |
|-------|-------|---------|------|
| 500 g | 350 g | 100 g   | 10 g |

> [!warning] Careful
> The pot is as hot as the oven.
